Final Chapter

Our Shared Future

The UAE Year of Family 2026 — and what comes after the sands.

In 2026, the United Arab Emirates dedicates the year to the family. Under the slogan Growing in Unity, the Year of Family invites everyone — parents and children, grandparents and grandchildren, neighbours and communities — to strengthen the bonds that hold society together.

Its purpose is simple and serious at once: to strengthen family bonds and equip families to grow with stability and confidence, shaping a future rooted in shared identity and worthy of our children's ambitions. It is the national echo of what these photographs already show — roots give us identity, bonds give us belonging, and branches give us tomorrow.

Growing in Unity

The slogan is not decoration — it is the mechanism. The Year of Family asks families to grow together: values modelled at home, connections strengthened in daily life, and support extended to every stage of family life. Unity here means one thing above all — that no family should have to grow alone.

Roots in action

Practising and modelling positive values through small, repeatable actions — the traditions of chapter one, lived daily.

Bonds in action

Everyday behaviours that make positive interactions more frequent — meals, visits, presence — the rituals of chapter two.

Branches in action

Clear guidance and accessible tools for daily challenges — the support systems of chapter three.

Families already answering the call

The UAE Year Of Award 2026 honoured individuals, families and organisations whose work embodies the year's values — judged on Impact, Sustainability and Inspiration. A few of the recognitions:

  • Salem Obaid Al Dhaheri Family — Al Ain · Main Award. A multi-generational family whose children carry the family's values into diplomacy, medicine, aviation, engineering and beyond; their "Sawainaha" series shares everyday parenting lessons with families across the UAE.
  • Mohammed Yousuf Al Rais Family — Sharjah · Collective Contribution. Through the Sheikha Hind bint Maktoum Family Programme, including "Dubai Weddings", which has helped more than 1,000 families begin married life with less financial strain.
  • Community Development Authority — Dubai · Government Champion. Transformed City Centre Mirdif into a more family-friendly destination. Majid Al Futtaim Group — Private Sector Champion, for initiatives supporting family life at scale. Meera Mubarak Al Ketbi — Civil Sector Champion, for turning personal hardship into community support through "Khayrat Al Dhaid".

Photography: UAE Year Of Award 2026 · uaeyearof.ae

Beyond 2026

The Year of Family is not a one-time campaign. It marks the beginning of a sustained national effort to embed family wellbeing into long-term policy and service design — continuing through the National Family Growth Agenda 2031, which keeps family growth and stability at the centre of national planning for years to come.

For this website, that is the deepest meaning of its title: beyond the sands is not an escape from the desert, but a direction — forward, together, generation after generation.

One story, three threads

Roots Values repeated until they become identity. Bonds Presence, meals and memory that make a house a home. Branches Growth supported, so every generation can reach higher.

“The future of the UAE that we are working for today begins with the family; from it, generations are built, and through it we safeguard our culture, values and identity.”

— His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, President of the UAE

Beyond the sands, every footprint is a family. Every family is a future.
