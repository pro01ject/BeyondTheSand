The idea behind this story

A family is a landscape of its own

This website is a journey through the families of the United Arab Emirates — told through the landscapes they inherit, the bonds they build, and the generations they raise.

It was inspired by the UAE Year of Family 2026, a national year dedicated to strengthening family bonds under the slogan Growing in Unity. The initiative reminds us that families grow through care, connection and everyday presence — and that what we nurture in our homes today shapes our communities and our nation tomorrow.

Every photograph and story here is real: families in Ras Al Khaimah reviving pottery, chefs in Hatta feeding their neighbours, children learning on a farm in Sweihan, an aunt and niece discovering friendship in Fujairah. They remind us that the Emirati family story is still being written — one meal, one craft, one generation at a time.

The national frame

The Year of Family is organised around three priorities — Roots, Bonds and Branches — the same three ideas that shape this website.

Why this story matters

Families are where identity is rehearsed before it is performed. The values a child absorbs at a kitchen table become the instincts of an adult; the stories told beside a fire become the memory of a nation.

When a craft is relearned, when a recipe survives a migration, when a grandmother's chair is still sat in — the desert does not just hold footprints. It holds futures.
