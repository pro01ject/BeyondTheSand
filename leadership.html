The Union

How the Federation Was Formed

18 July 1971

The rulers of six emirates — all except Ras Al Khaimah — resolved to form a union.

2 December 1971

The United Arab Emirates was founded; Sheikh Zayed was elected its first President.

10 February 1972

Ras Al Khaimah acceded — all seven emirates united in one federation.

From these roots and this trunk grew seven branches — one for every emirate, each carrying its own ruler, its own story, and the same shared future.

The Future

A United Nation

Seven trunks, one root system. This tree is not a family tree — it is a picture of a union deliberately built: seven rulers who chose federation over separation, and generations who have kept that choice alive.

Every branch still reaches the same soil. The federation the founders planted in 1971 continues to grow — not because it was declared once, but because it is renewed every day in the homes, schools and communities of all seven emirates.

— Beyond the Sands
