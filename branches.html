Chapter Three

Our Branches

Where families grow — children learning, parents supported, and a future prepared one generation at a time.

A tree is judged by its roots and its bonds — but it lives or dies by its branches. Children grow where they are allowed to wonder, to learn through play, and to know they are supported.

Growing is never a family's private burden. The official Year of Family framework calls this Branches: clear guidance and accessible support that help families navigate daily challenges with confidence — from young couples beginning a home, to parents and caregivers, to elders who are themselves the roots.

The Farm is a School and a Playground

In Sweihan, a family farm is doing what schools cannot: it teaches by letting children run. Saleh, five, and his sister Sara, three, spend their days among pigeons, goats and Jersey cows — asking questions no worksheet would think to ask, like what a caterpillar becomes (his answer: a pterodactyl).

Their mother Jessica, who runs the farm's catering with their father, describes their education as happening "both at home and on the farm" — outdoors, through firsthand experience, through play. The siblings teach each other constantly: Sara's curiosity about what goats eat leads the conversation; Saleh's imagination turns a caterpillar into a dinosaur.

The farm's knowledge — which seeds take, which trees shade, which bull to avoid — is itself an inheritance, passed father to child across generations. The children are not just visiting it. They are already part of it.

Behind these stories stands a national commitment: the National Family Growth Agenda 2031, launched in 2025, aims to strengthen family stability and wellbeing through policy, community programmes and everyday support — so that strong families remain the foundation of a strong nation.

The National Family Growth Agenda 2031

Announced at the UAE Government Annual Meetings in November 2025, the Agenda is the long-term framework behind the Year of Family. It focuses on increasing family stability and growth through three pathways: policies and programmes, behavioural interventions, and reproductive health — supported by a national task force of more than twenty government bodies.

The Year of Family 2026 is its flagship year: a period of awareness, programmes and everyday encouragement, designed so that what begins in 2026 continues well beyond it.

Source: UAE Government · WAM / u.ae

  • Family First — priority parking for expectant mothers, family seating on public transport, and Family and Child Oasis Rooms.
  • Nafis updates — children's allowances uncapped, support extended to children of Emirati mothers, programme extended to 2040.
  • Emirati Family Data — simplified family information services through the Ministry of Justice and ICP.
  • Community programmes — marriage support, flexible work policies, parental benefits and family-friendly initiatives nationwide.
